The AMMP Reporting Studio lets you build professional PDF reports from your portfolio's energy data: for clients, stakeholders or your own team. Save layouts as templates and reuse them each month. Open it via **Reporting** under **Commercial Operations** in the AMMP OS Portal.

## The layout

The Reporting Studio has three areas.

### Report configuration (top)

These settings apply to every chart and table in the report:

- **Date range:** a quick range or custom start and end dates.
- **Asset:** search and select the asset or assets to include.

> [!NOTE]
> Dates and times are shown in your browser's time zone. AMMP data is stored in UTC and converted for display.

### Components (left)

| Group | Components |
| --- | --- |
| Text and content | Text block, image, period info |
| Metadata | Metadata card |
| Data visualisations | Line chart, bar chart, table, aggregation |

### Report canvas

- **Page tabs:** switch pages, or add one with **+ Add**.
- **Layout options:** switch between **Grid** and **Align** mode at the top right.
- **Header:** content that appears at the top of each page, such as a logo, title and period.
- **Content area:** the body of the report.
- **Footer:** content at the bottom of each page, such as notes.

## Building a report

### 1. Set the report configuration

Choose the date range and assets first, before adding charts.

### 2. Add components

Click a component in the left panel and drag it onto the canvas.

- **Text block:** type directly into it.
- **Image:** upload a PNG, JPG or SVG, such as your logo.
- **Period info:** shows the configured date range automatically.
- **Metadata card:** choose the field to show, such as Asset Name, Long Name or Total PV Power.

### 3. Add data visualisations

First set the data in the **Data Visualizations** section:

1. **Endpoint type:** Energy, Power, Performance or Financial data.
2. **Data types:** one or more, for example PV Energy (Solar).
3. **Data interval:** for example daily or monthly.

Then add a **line chart**, **bar chart**, **table** or **aggregation** (total, average, minimum, maximum or count).

> [!TIP]
> Select several data types from the same endpoint to compare them in one chart.

### 4. Add pages

Click **+ Add** next to the page tabs. Use **Grid** mode for a structured layout, and **Align** mode for precise positioning.

## Templates

**Save a template:** click **Save Template**, enter a name and description, and click **Save Template**. The template name then shows at the top of the page.

**Load a template:** click **Load Template** and pick one from the list, or start a new one.

> [!TIP]
> Save your regular monthly or quarterly reports as templates to cut reporting time.

## Exporting to PDF

Click **Export to PDF**. The report is generated with all components and data, and downloaded to your device.
