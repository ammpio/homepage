Google Reporting lets you build reports from your monitoring data in Google Looker Studio, for operational analysis, customer updates or investor reporting. Open it via **External Apps → Google Reporting** in the AMMP OS Portal. The welcome page links to your report templates and to Looker Studio's help.

For PDF reports built inside AMMP OS, see [Reporting Studio](./reporting-studio.md).

## Creating your first report

1. **Find your templates.** Click **See Your Templates** at the bottom of the welcome page. Looker Studio opens with your available templates.
2. **Open a template.** It opens in View mode. Click **Edit** at the top right to switch to Edit mode.
3. **Make a copy.** Click the three-dot menu in the top bar and choose **Make a copy**. Work on the copy.

In your copy you can change logos, branding, text boxes and other visual elements.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To change the data in charts or tables, or add specific KPIs, contact [support@ammp.io](mailto:support@ammp.io) rather than editing the data sources yourself.
