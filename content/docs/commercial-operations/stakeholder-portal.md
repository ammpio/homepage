The AMMP Stakeholder Portal is a version of the [Monitoring Center](./monitoring-center.md) with dashboards tailored to your end customers and investors. It gives them a professional, transparent view of their sites under your branding.

## Who uses it

Give clients, investors or EPC partners the **Limited Viewer** profile. When they sign in, they go straight to the Stakeholder Portal and can't see the rest of AMMP OS. Your own team (Standard Users) can open it too. See [User management](./user-management.md) to invite users.

## Using it

The Stakeholder Portal works the same way as the Monitoring Center: the same sidebar, time range picker, selectors and panel exports. Follow the [Monitoring Center guide](./monitoring-center.md).

## Getting help

Email [support@ammp.io](mailto:support@ammp.io), or hover over the Help icon at the bottom left and choose **Support**.
