# Apply Luminous Glass across the AMMP site

## Goal
Extend the homepage’s Luminous Glass visual language across every AMMP page while preserving all existing copy, facts, links, imagery, forms, tabs, comparisons, and interactions.

## Site-wide visual system
- Use the same airy porcelain canvas, restrained amber and softened magenta ambient glows, and generous whitespace throughout the site.
- Restyle the shared header, menus, footer, and closing call-to-action as refined translucent surfaces that remain clear and readable.
- Turn shared content surfaces—feature cards, steps, statistics, FAQs, comparison tables, quotes, forms, integration logos, and article cards—into consistent frosted-glass elements with subtle borders and layered shadows.
- Give page introductions a recognizable Luminous Glass composition, with controlled ambient light and optional floating glass content where a page already provides supporting content.
- Preserve the established Inter typography, pink brand accents, content hierarchy, and alternating section rhythm without introducing dark sections.

## Page-specific coverage
- Adapt interactive product and integrations tabs, search, filters, pricing modules, and comparison tables to the same glass treatment while keeping their behavior unchanged.
- Apply the treatment to blog listing and article pages, contact/demo forms and meeting embed, use-case pages, comparison pages, legal pages, and remaining company/product pages.
- Keep screenshots, videos, logos, and customer imagery intact, presenting them within cleaner glass frames where appropriate.

## Motion and accessibility
- Reuse the restrained reveal and ambient motion language already established on the homepage for shared page sections and repeated cards.
- Preserve strong contrast, visible focus states, readable tables, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Keep effects lighter on mobile to avoid clipping, visual noise, or performance regressions.

## Technical details
- Centralize most changes in the shared section kit, navigation shell, brand grid, and global semantic utilities so all routes remain visually consistent.
- Add only small route-level class changes for unique layouts that cannot inherit the shared treatment.
- Verify representative pages across desktop and mobile, including navigation, product tabs, integration search, pricing comparison, forms, video, and blog articles, then confirm clean diagnostics.
